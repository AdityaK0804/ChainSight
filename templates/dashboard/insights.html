How the models work

local_shippingDelay Risk Model

A RandomForest classifier trained on 2,000 synthetic supply chain records. For each active order, it takes the supplier's on-time delivery history, lead time, days remaining until expected delivery, past delay count, and country-level logistics risk. It outputs a probability (0–1) that the order will be delayed, which maps to a low/medium/high label. Probabilities above 0.65 trigger a critical alert.

inventory_2Stockout Risk Model

A GradientBoosting regressor that outputs a continuous risk score from 0 to 1. Key inputs are how many days of stock remain compared to the supplier's lead time, how close current stock is to the reorder point, and the supplier's overall reliability. Scores above 0.75 trigger a critical stockout alert; above 0.5 trigger a warning.
